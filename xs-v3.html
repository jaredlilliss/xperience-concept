The First Space Nation

UAE’s long-term vision for the future

Guided by visionary leadership, the UAE is transitioning from a resourced based to a hiTech economy driven by the space program. Early achievements have helped form a structured, long-horizon strategy with advanced missions, strong governance, and a developing space sector. This strategy propels UAE towards leadership in Exploration, R&D and Investment developing exciting opportunties for the global space economy.

From historic vision to structured roadmap

A 1974 meeting with NASA set the foundation for UAE's vision for space. Under the visionary leadership of UAE government this vision evolved into a clear mandate: Position the UAE as a global leader in Space Exploration, Research & Investment.

The UAE now advances sovereign missions, contributes to the Lunar Gateway, and invests in technologies, governance and infrastructure that enable long-term human presence beyond Earth.

Why an ecosystem is essential

To sustain this trajectory, the UAE requires a robust local ecosystem which develops talent, aligns regulations, attracts private capital, and accelerates technology transfer into the country.

XperienceSpace complements UAE's vision by providing a structured collaboration platform which develops industry & community, making UAE’s long-term space vision economically tangible and globally accessible.

Core Framework

4 Pillars powering XperienceSpace

XperienceSpace initiative structures programs around four interdependent pillars. Each pillar channels community energy, builds capabilities, and supports UAE’s official space initiatives with practical, investment-ready mechanisms.

Public Engagement & Community

Raising awareness and inspiring innovation by addressing Earth’s challenges, creating new opportunities for UAE’s growth in the space economy. A space for Emiratis to touch their dreams.

Education & Talent Pipeline

Developing future leaders by applying UAE-driven solutions to global and space challenges, strengthening national expertise and resilience.

Industry Development & Support

Creating initiatives to develop cutting-edge solutions addressing local and global needs, driving UAE’s leadership in space and hiTech industries.

Research & Development

Developing products & services needed to solve Earth’s sustainability challenges and build a competitive, future-ready space sector for UAE.

Strategic Trajectory

From programs to a fully functioning UAE Space City

What we do

  • Promote & support UAE national programs for space, economic growth and comunity development.
  • Translate UAE space objectives into clear engagement and investment pathways.
  • Help global partners localize capabilities, IP and operations in the UAE.
  • Facilitate collaboration frameworks that respect UAE regulations and sovereign oversight.

What we are not

  • Not a regulator, government agency or operator of national missions.
  • Not a replacement for UAE institutions or official strategies.
  • Not a speculative venture; programs are tied to measurable readiness and real capabilities.
